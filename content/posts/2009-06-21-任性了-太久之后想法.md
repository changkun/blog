{{% en %}}
After being willful for so long,

I begin to miss who I was at the start.

Going around in circles,

yet somehow

I can't find my way back to the beginning.
{{% /en %}}

{{% zh %}}
任性了 太久之后

我开始怀念 最初的我

兜兜转转

却

怎么也回不到 起点
{{% /zh %}}
